Where It Began

From a men's salon
to a growing experience.

What began as a men's salon in 2015 has grown into a wellness and relaxation experience across multiple locations. Starting in Baniyas, Abu Dhabi, the business expanded to serve more communities across the UAE.

Built on experience, professional care and a commitment to customer comfort, our journey continues. In 2026, we bring that experience to Umm Salal Mohammed, Qatar, with Prime Relax Spa for Men.
